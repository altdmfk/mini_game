How To Play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Hit the ENTER key to submit.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word:

W
Green: Letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
I
Yellow: Letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
U
Gray: Letter is not in the word in any spot.
Controls: Use your physical keyboard (A-Z, Enter, Backspace) or the on-screen buttons.
